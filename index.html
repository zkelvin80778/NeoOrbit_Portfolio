Our Mission

NeoOrbit is a student-led innovation lab that engineers sustainable technologies for both terrestrial and extraterrestrial applications. We design resilient energy systems built for the future of the Earth—and beyond.

Projects

3D-Printed Solar Concentrator

This project tackles the unique challenge of habitat temperature regulation for future Martian missions. Utilizing thermoelectric generators, 3D-printed components, and open-source control systems, it aims to provide practical solutions for off-world sustainability.
Status: TRL 3

rPET Machine V0

A compact, open-source system designed to convert post-consumer plastic bottles into usable 3D printing filament. It features modular thermal extrusion, custom electronics, and a focus on educational accessibility to close the loop on local plastic waste.
Status: TRL 4
